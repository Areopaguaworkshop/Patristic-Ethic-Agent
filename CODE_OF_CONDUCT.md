# Code of Conduct

This project discusses ethics, religion, and personal harm. Disagreement is
expected; harassment is not.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not a person's character or faith.
- Assume good faith, but ask for evidence when a claim matters.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's messages, identity, health
  information, or credentials.
- Do not use the project to harass, threaten, or expose anyone.
- Keep pastoral and crisis discussions careful. Do not present this project as
  a substitute for emergency services or professional care.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, sexual attention,
deliberate exposure of private data, and sustained disruption are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may block accounts
for behaviour that violates this policy. Report a concern privately through
GitHub's private vulnerability reporting or a maintainer contact listed on the
repository profile. Reports are handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
