What was searched

  • Google Scholar: 20 queries, 232 result rows, 184 unique; result pages fetched 23 Oct 2026. Google Scholar rate-limited further queries (HTTP 204). The browser extension was not connected, so Scholar pages were fetched by HTTP rather than an interactive browser session.
  • Crossref: 24 bibliographic queries, 720 rows, 480 unique; metadata and DOIs fetched 23 Oct 2026.
  • OpenAlex: topic and title checks, plus metadata verification for core records.
  • Manual core records: companions, handbooks, and monographs checked against publisher or DOI records where available.

What was excluded

  • Primary texts, translations, and critical editions.
  • Book reviews and review notes.
  • Works outside 1986–2026.
  • Generic patristic theology with no ethical or moral theme, except explicitly labelled background works.
  • Quotations and interpretive notes: the bibliography contains neither.
Limits. This is a working bibliography, not a systematic review. Google Scholar stopped serving pages after 20 queries. ATLA, IxTheo, and Brepols were not searched directly. Citation counts are omitted because they are volatile and uneven across fields. Metadata for 70 records without a DOI comes from Google Scholar or a manual check; verify before citing. 58 records have rights_status: unknown; 82 are library-access records and 5 are open access.
Coverage notes. PEA-P03 (terrorism and extremist recruitment) has no dedicated secondary study in this collection; the P03 list is general war, violence, and peace scholarship. PEA-P06 (idolatry) and PEA-P09 (envy and ambition) are thinner than the other principles. Syriac and Latin coverage is thinner than Greek. The canonical JSONL records these gaps as draft-unreviewed records, not as evidence.
